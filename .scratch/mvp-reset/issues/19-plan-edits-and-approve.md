# Plan tab: resize, remove and Approve

Type: build
Status: open
Assignee:
Blocked by: [/plan-week-cal and the Plan tab grid](18-plan-week-cal-and-plan-grid.md)

## What to build

Tap a meal on the grid to resize it (½ / ¾ / 1 / 1¼ / 1½ servings) or remove it; totals update live. Swap and move are asked of the Agent. An Approve button marks the Meal Plan approved.

## Done when

- Resize and remove persist and update day totals
- Approve marks the week approved (the Shopping list is built in #20)
