# Language: English and Spanish

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by:

## Question

The second Member speaks Spanish. Which language is data stored in, what does each Member see, and what has to be done in the MVP vs. later?

## Answer

- **All data is stored in English**: Food Library, Pantry, Meal Plan, Grocery List and Calorie Logs, so food data is in one language and matches the English-only USDA lookup.
- **MVP:** English only. All screen text lives in a single translation file rather than being written into the pages, so Spanish can be added later without touching every screen.
- **Phase 3 (when the second Member joins):** each Member picks their language. She sees the app in Spanish and the Agent chats, recommends and reflects in Spanish, while understanding Spanish or mixed text she types.
- **Her own wording is kept.** When she saves an item in Spanish (for example "pechuga de pollo"), it is stored under its English name ("chicken breast") **and** her original Spanish text is saved with it. She sees what she wrote, not a translation back from English. You see the English name.
