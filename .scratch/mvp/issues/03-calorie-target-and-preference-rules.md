# How are Calorie Targets set, and how strict are Preferences?

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by:

## Question

(1) How does each Member's daily Calorie Target get set in the MVP: typed in by hand, or calculated from body stats and a chosen deficit? Weight tracking is out of scope. Does the target differ by day, for example on weekends?
(2) Preferences are dislikes, allergies and diet. Which of these are hard rules the Agent must never break, and which are soft? When the two Members' Preferences conflict on a shared meal, what happens?

## Answer

### Calorie Target
- **MVP:** typed in by hand, the same every day. The primary Member works out his own number.
- **Phase 2:** calculated from age, sex, height, weight, the chosen deficit and logged workouts (workouts add burned calories, and the Meal Plan is built for the resulting deficit). This is so the second Member never has to calculate anything herself.
- **Safe floor (from #08):** a hand-typed target below 1,500/day (men) or 1,200/day (women) shows a warning but is allowed. Phase 2's calculated target keeps the same floor.
- **Weigh-ins (phase 2):** a weekly reminder by default. Daily entries are allowed, and when there are several in a week the 7-day average is used. The reminder frequency is an editable setting.

### Preferences
- **Allergies:** always hard, with no switch. They're written into the Agent's rules from day one, even though neither Member has any today, so the app is ready to become a product.
- **Diets:** each has a hard/soft switch. The MVP only needs a simple list. Specific diet options (vegetarian, keto, etc.) come in phase 2.
- **Hard dislike:** the Member would never eat it. It never appears in their Portion.
- **Soft dislike:** the Member doesn't want it as a noticeable part of a dish, but it's fine blended in where they can't taste it. The Agent doesn't feature it, but may suggest working it in unnoticed when there's a good reason, and always says so openly (for example "Avocado would help your healthy fats. How about blending it into the sauce?").
- **Breaking your own hard rule:** if a Member logs or plans something that breaks one of their own hard rules (an allergy or a hard diet), the app **warns but allows it**, because the Calorie Log records what was actually eaten. The Agent never *suggests* anything that breaks a hard rule.
- **Shared meals (phase 3):** if either Member has a hard rule, it applies to the whole meal. Soft dislikes are handled with per-person add-ons (for example avocado on her hamburger only).
