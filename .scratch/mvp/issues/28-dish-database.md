## Question

Split out of #22 (2026-10-01). The Member wants a "recipe database" so that cooks can be scheduled (#27) and planned against the Pantry. CONTEXT.md already has **Dish**: a named meal made of ingredients with amounts, a total prep time and the Kitchen Tools it needs, with no steps. The map lists "Recipes" as out of scope, and "recipes with cooking steps" as a future phase. Dishes show up in many tickets (Favorites and Dish feedback in #05, prep time in #06, Kitchen Tools in #07, the Food Library in #20), but no ticket says where they are stored, browsed or edited.

Open questions:
- **What a Dish record holds:**
  - ingredients and amounts, for how many Portions;
  - calories per Portion, calculated from the ingredients;
  - prep time and Kitchen Tools;
  - its Ingredient / Ready to eat tag once it's cooked;
  - its rating and feedback.
- **How Dishes are added:**
  - by the Agent when it plans;
  - saved from a cook (#27);
  - typed in by hand;
  - from a Favorite.
- **Where the Dish list lives:** a page or a section, with search and filters.
- **Editing a Dish:** what changes for meals that are already planned, versus the next time.
- **The name:** keep "Dish", or bring in "recipe" now while it still has no steps? Then update CONTEXT.md and the map's Out of scope line.
